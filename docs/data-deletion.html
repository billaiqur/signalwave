Data Deletion Request

Last updated: July 26, 2026

If you've connected a Facebook Page, Instagram profile, or YouTube channel to Signalwave — or if you're someone whose public comment was collected because you commented on a connected profile — you can request that we delete your data at any time.

What gets deleted

Once a deletion request is confirmed, we remove:

  • Any comments, commenter names, and post details collected from your connected profile(s)
  • Sentiment scores, harassment flags, and moderation history tied to that data
  • Access tokens allowing Signalwave to read further data from your account

We may retain minimal records where required by law (for example, financial or fraud-prevention records), as described in our Privacy Policy.

How long we keep data, and when it's deleted

  • If your account goes idle for a year — we'll email you asking whether you'd like to keep your data. If you reply, we act on your instructions. If we don't hear back, we delete your data 30 days after that follow-up.
  • If your account stays active — we don't delete anything automatically. Instead, you can clean up older data yourself at any time, choosing to remove content older than 2 years, 5 years, or 7 years from your dashboard settings.
  • If you close your account — we delete everything within 24 hours of confirmed closure. This deletion is permanent and cannot be undone or recovered.

How to request deletion right now

If you're logged in, the fastest option is Settings → Data & retention, where you can clean up old data yourself or close your account outright (deleted within 24 hours, permanently). Otherwise:

  1. Disconnect your account (fastest option). From your Signalwave dashboard, go to Settings → Connected Accounts and click "Disconnect" next to the relevant profile. This immediately revokes our access and stops any further data collection.
  2. Submit a deletion request. Use the form below, or email us directly at Laiqur@blackfin-cybers.io with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and the profile name or email associated with your account.
  3. Confirmation. We'll confirm your request by email and complete the deletion within 30 days, in line with our data retention policy.
